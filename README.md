#### Currently, she focuses on research involving Geographic Information Systems (GIS), remote sensing, hydrological modeling, and machine learning for flood hazard assessment and water resources management. Her research interests include hydrology, flood susceptibility mapping, flood risk assessment, spatial analysis, and the application of Earth observation data for hydrological studies. She has experience in integrating geospatial analysis, hydrological simulation, machine learning techniques, and environmental datasets to support flood hazard assessment and disaster risk reduction. She is also open to research collaboration in hydrology, GIS, remote sensing, machine learning, and disaster risk assessment.
